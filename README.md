# Antonio Claudio

**PhD Researcher | Robotics & Machine Learning**

I am an Electrical Engineer graduated from the Federal University of Maranhão (UFMA), Brazil, currently pursuing my PhD in Clermont-Ferrand, France at Institut Pascal.

Currently, my research focuses on **Transfer learning for robotic applications**, exploring how knowledge and learned skills can be transferred across robotic platforms.

### What I am interested in

- 🤖 Transfer learning for robotics
- 🧠 Reinforcement learning
- 🌍 Sim-to-real transfer
- 💻 Robotics software and machine learning
